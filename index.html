Oxidation of Methanol

Formation of Formaldehyde

Reaction Equation

2CH₃OH + O₂ → 2HCHO + 2H₂O

(Catalyst: Cu or Ag, Heat)

Explanation

Methanol (CH₃OH) undergoes catalytic oxidation using copper (Cu) or silver (Ag) as a catalyst at high temperatures. This process breaks the C-H bond and the O-H bond, resulting in the formation of Formaldehyde (HCHO) and water. This is a primary alcohol oxidation reaction.

Safety Note: Formaldehyde is a toxic gas. This experiment must be conducted in a well-ventilated area or fume hood.
